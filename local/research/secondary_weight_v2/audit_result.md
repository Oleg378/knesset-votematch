## q5 in detail

Fitting bills share one thing: the state funds a body that keeps the heritage or culture of a particular community or movement —
Tunisian/Djerba heritage center, Iranian heritage council, national authority for Russian-speaking Jews' culture, Chabad heritage,
WWII Jewish fighter heritage. That is exactly the statement's question. Misfit bills (0.37): the two Nation-State Law repeal bills
(0.35) are about the constitutional status of the state's Jewish character, which q4 already asks; 2211131 (0.02) is about the size of
government. Recommended remedy: relabel — remove 164 from the repeal bills (they keep 165 and still count on q4) and from 2211131
(keeps 279). No rewrite needed. Optional wording tweak to cover the cultural authority: «The state should not create or fund new
heritage centers or cultural bodies for individual communities, such as Tunisian, Iranian or Russian-speaking Jews, even if their
traditions are less well preserved.»

## Patterns

- Repeal or reversal of a big symbolic law (Nation-State Law, Citizenship Law) gets a secondary label for every value it touches.
- Bills whose main topic has its own direction leak into a neighbouring statement: debtor protection → tax enforcement, privacy,
  challenging authorities; terror sanctions → pensions, health funds; reservist relief → debtors; KKL → media ownership.
- Omnibus or area bills (Kiryat Shmona, senior-citizen dignity, Greater Jerusalem).
- Keyword matches: «committee» → abortion committees; «war» → emergency powers; «minors» → sex offences; «equality» → LGBT.
- K24/K25 resubmissions count one misfit two or three times.

Possible rule: drop a secondary label when the bill's main topic is a direction with its own statement (or same family, e.g.
277x, 130x). It catches the Nation-State, collection, terror-sanction and reservist cases, not keyword, omnibus or main-label
misfits; flag any secondary bill with share > 0.2 for review.
