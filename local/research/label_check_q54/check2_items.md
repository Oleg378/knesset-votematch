Direction «Denial of benefits and rights as a sanction» — +: Denies or revokes benefits, allowances, licenses or other state rights from people as a sanction: those involved in terror and their families, illegal residents or infiltrators, boycott supporters, convicted officials. −: Restores such benefits or rights, or limits the state's power to deny them as a sanction.
