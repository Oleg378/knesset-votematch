## C1
Statement (q36): The state should reduce its support for settlements in Judea and Samaria (the West Bank), even if this makes life there harder for settlers.
Direction «State support for settlements» — +: Increases state funding, planning, regularisation, services or security for settlements in Judea and Samaria. −: Reduces them.
