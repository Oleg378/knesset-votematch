**Verdict: the asymmetry is partly real, but it is about how strong the price is, more than what kind it is.** The price types are close: 58% of right-agree and 47% of left-agree statements carry a harm or rights price. Counterexamples: q36, q39, q5 are left-agree with harm prices; q2, q13, q18, q24, q61 are right-agree with procedural prices. The real gap is strength: all five severe (3) prices are right-agree (q3, q6, q10, q49, q54); no left-agree price reaches 3. Left-agree prices average 1.37 against 2.08. Mirror pairs: q4 ("non-Jews feel less equal", 2) against q74 ("courts get grounds", 2 but abstract); q24/q61/q62 (2) against q1/q48/q80/q105 (1, "governing slows").

**Remedy:** give left-agree statements the real cost their opponents name (the elected mandate, religious freedom, the Jewish character). Do not soften true humanitarian costs.

**Change:** q1, q7, q40, q48, q64, q74, q75, q80, q96, q105 (balance); q12, q24, q33, q49, q54, q83 (loaded wording); q21, q69, q72, q23 (missing or weak price); q88, q90 (consensus wording, double). After these fixes, left-agree prices average 1.79 against 2.08. 16 statements exceed 25 words.
