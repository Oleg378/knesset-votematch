Statement (q70): Public institutions such as hospitals and army bases should follow Jewish religious rules, like no leavened bread on Passover, even if non-observant people are restricted.
Direction «Jewish religious rules in public institutions» — +: Makes hospitals, army bases, state bodies or public places follow more Jewish religious rules (kashrut, no leavened bread on Passover, Shabbat). −: Makes them follow fewer such rules.
