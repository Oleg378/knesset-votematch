## T1
Statement (q72): Israelis, including Jews, should be allowed to buy land in Judea and Samaria (the West Bank), even if this makes a future territorial agreement harder.
Direction «Jewish land rights in Judea and Samaria» — +: Expands rights of Israelis/Jews to buy, hold or reclaim land in Judea and Samaria (repeal of Jordanian purchase ban, pre-1948 land restoration). −: Restricts such purchase or reclamation.
Bill: הצעת חוק הגנה על מחזיקים במקרקעין ביהודה ושומרון, התשפ"ב-2022
