# Предложения: понятнее сформулировать направления для Jev

Цифры «НП / спорные / верные» — строки Jev с applies ≥ 0,6 на 50 законах (тесты 3–5) по вердиктам эксперта. Ответь по номерам: апрув / реджект.

**1. [95]** 14 неприменимых / 4 спорных / 1 верное — срабатывает почти везде; Jev читает как «государство отвечает перед гражданами» вообще
  - было: **Liability of the state and institutions to citizens** — +: Widens the liability of the state and institutions toward citizens. / −: Narrows that liability.
  - стало: **State liability to pay damages** — +: Makes it easier to sue the state or public institutions for damages, or widens what they must compensate (tort liability). / −: Narrows the state's or public institutions' liability to pay damages.

**2. [312]** 7 / 8 / 6 — путается с любыми новыми задачами ведомств
  - было: **Obligations of state bodies to citizens** — +: Imposes more duties on state bodies to inform and serve citizens, or gives citizens more guarantees and a stronger position toward the administration and providers. / −: Imposes fewer such duties and guarantees.
  - стало: **Service duties of agencies toward the public** — +: Obliges state agencies to inform, answer, meet deadlines or keep service standards toward members of the public. / −: Removes or relaxes such service duties toward the public.

**3. [149]** 6 / 6 / 7 — Jev ставит «−» любому ужесточению наказаний
  - было: **Personal liberty and autonomy** — +: Gives individuals more freedom of personal choice, private conduct and privacy. / −: Increases state interference or prohibitions on private conduct.
  - стало: **Freedom of private, self-regarding conduct** — +: Allows adults more private choices that mainly affect themselves (lifestyle, body, consensual private behavior). / −: Prohibits or restricts such private, self-regarding choices. Penalties for harming others do not count.

**4. [296]** 6 / 4 / 4 — срабатывает на любое упоминание зарубежного опыта
  - было: **Alignment with international standards** — +: Brings Israeli law closer to international standards. / −: Moves it further from them.
  - стало: **Adoption of international treaties and standards** — +: The bill explicitly adopts or implements a specific international treaty, convention or standard. Mentioning other countries' practice does not count. / −: The bill departs from or withdraws from a specific international treaty or standard.

**5. [40]** 5 / 4 / 2
  - было: **Administrative burden on citizens** — +: Adds bureaucracy and requirements for citizens. / −: Reduces bureaucracy and requirements for citizens.
  - стало: **Paperwork required from individuals** — +: Individuals must file more forms, documents or applications, or go through more steps, to get something from the state. / −: Individuals need fewer forms, documents or steps; things happen automatically.

**6. [353]** 5 / 1 / 4 — применяется к государственным льготам, где договора нет

  - стало: **Mandatory protective terms in private contracts** — +: Adds mandatory terms protecting the weaker side in contracts between private parties (consumers, tenants, insured persons, borrowers). / −: Removes such mandatory terms; the stronger side has more contractual freedom.

**7. [370]** 5 / 3 / 2 — дублирует отраслевые направления (2, 11, 253, 34, 356)

  - стало: **Public services across several sectors** — +: Expands public services in several sectors at once (e.g. a general package of health, transport and utility services). For a single sector, the specific direction applies instead. / −: Reduces public services across several sectors at once.
